# Product Intelligence AI

### Turning messy product data into structured, validated and decision-ready intelligence.

Product Intelligence AI is a web-based product intelligence platform designed to transform incomplete, inconsistent and unstructured product information into structured product data with normalization, validation, evidence and quality scoring.

## 🎯 Problem

Industrial and commercial product information is often scattered across different formats and may contain:

- Incomplete product attributes
- Inconsistent formats
- Unstandardized values
- Missing specifications
- Unclear product classification
- Data-quality issues

Simply extracting information is not enough.

Businesses need to know whether the information is structured, consistent and trustworthy.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

Product Intelligence AI follows an intelligence pipeline:

**Input → Understand → Normalize → Validate → Score → Intelligence**

The platform transforms product information into a structured product profile and provides quality and intelligence signals to help users understand the reliability of the data.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 🔍 Product Analysis

Analyze product information and generate a structured product profile.

### ⚙️ Smart Normalization

Standardize inconsistent product attributes such as:

- Dimensions
- Currency
- Color
- Compatibility

### 🛡️ Validation Engine

Validate extracted product information and identify potential data-quality issues.

### 📊 Intelligence & Quality Scoring

Generate:

- Product Intelligence Score
- Data Quality Score
- Product Context Score

### 🧾 Evidence & Confidence

Provide supporting evidence and confidence information for extracted attributes.

### 📈 Dashboard

Monitor analyzed products, intelligence scores and data-quality information.

### 🔄 History & Comparison

Review previous analyses and compare products using different decision criteria.
